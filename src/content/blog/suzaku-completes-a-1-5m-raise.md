We are proud to announce the completion of a total raise of $1.5M to help [Avalanche](https://avax.network) L1s decentralize securely, and become the staking layer of the Avalanche ecosystem!

The funds were raised across our seed round, public sales, and grants from the [Avalanche Foundation](https://www.avax.network/about/foundation).

We feel fortunate to count long-term ecosystem players by our side: [Blizzard](https://www.blizzard.fund/), [Yield Yak](https://yieldyak.com/), [Tegridy Capital](https://tegridy.capital/), [50 Partners](https://www.50partners.fr/); and angels from [BENQI](https://benqi.fi/) founders, Yield Yak, [Swissborg](https://swissborg.com/) founders, [Chorus One](https://chorus.one/), [Stable Jack](https://stablejack.xyz/) founders.

A special thank you to Avalanche for their continuous support through programs like [Codebase](https://codebase.avax.network/), [InfraBUIDL()](https://build.avax.network/grants/infrabuidl), and [Retro9000](https://retro9000.avax.network/), and to amazing Web3 launchpads for hosting our public sales [Swissborg](https://swissborg.com/), [Seedify](https://seedify.fund/), [Colony](https://colony.io/)!

This is only the beginning of a great journey.
