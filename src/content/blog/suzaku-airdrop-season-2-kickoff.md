Suzaku TGE is just around the corner, August 13th! Along with the TGE, participants of Season 1 will be able to claim their SUZ airdrop.

Season 1 has been impressive, with nearly 10 billion Suzaku points, more than 10,000 eligible users, and an average airdrop APY of 80%!

This airdrop is just the beginning of the Suzaku journey. Season 2 is kicking off with new integrations and new ways to farm points!

## Suzaku Season 1 Airdrop

The snapshot for the airdrop was taken on **July 31st at 15:00 UTC**, and **more than 10,000 users** are eligible for the **Season 1 SUZ airdrop**!

We're distributing **4,750,000 SUZ (4.75% of the total supply)** to Suzaku points holders and partner communities (Swissborg, Colony, Stable Jack). An additional **89,221 SUZ** allocation goes to Suzaku community programs such as the London Hackathon and AMAs.

Claims open on **August 13th**. **Check your eligibility now, and claim** at [app.suzaku.network/airdrop](https://app.suzaku.network/airdrop) shortly after TGE!

Your allocation comes in two parts:

- **Half (50%)** arrives as unlocked SUZ tokens. You can choose to stake them for sSUZ for a [special points boost](https://docs.suzaku.network/suz-token/suz-airdrops#airdrop-points-boost) for Season 2!
- **The other half (50%)** is delivered as **sSUZ (staked SUZ)** that vests linearly over **six months**

For small allocations though, we keep it simple: if your airdrop is **under 375 SUZ**, you'll receive it **entirely as unlocked SUZ**.

**Note:** Colony and Swissborg users will have to claim on those platforms directly.

## How were allocations calculated?

We did our best to ensure that everyone in the Suzaku community receives a reward. For this purpose, 0.5% of the supply was distributed exclusively to the Suzaku Rush participants (June 19th to July 31st, 2025), while 4.25% was distributed for all contributions made throughout Season 1 (including the Rush).

## What to do with your SUZ?

At launch, there will already be **2 ways** to make use of your SUZ:

- **Staking SUZ** for [sSUZ](https://docs.suzaku.network/suz-token/suz-staking) and get a piece of the **1.5% of the total supply** that is distributed **in the first year**, as well as a [special points boost](https://docs.suzaku.network/suz-token/suz-airdrops#airdrop-points-boost) for airdrop recipients
- **Providing liquidity** to the SUZ/USDC pool on [Blackhole](https://blackhole.xyz/), and **maximizing your yield** with [Yield Yak](https://yieldyak.com/) auto-compounding strategies!

**Each of these options will earn you points and rewards**, and you can use them to participate in the Suzaku Season 2!

## Suzaku Season 2 Kickoff

Season 2 **started on July 31st**.

There are a few things to be aware of for this season:

- SUZ will be the critical piece of Suzaku going forward: it will have a **multiplier of 3x**, staking it for sSUZ will generate the **highest yield in SUZ**, and it will act as a **gateway to the Suzaku ecosystem rewards** (more on that later)
- You will be able to provide liquidity on [Blackhole](https://blackhole.xyz/) for SUZ/USDC and rsAVAX/AVAX to **earn points**
- The [LFJ](https://lfj.gg/) integration is **no longer active**: depositing liquidity for rsAVAX/AVAX and rBTC.b/BTC.b there won't earn you points for now

## Why should you stay around for Season 2?

Season 2 is set to be ever more exciting, with the **first Suzaku-secured L1s launching in the coming months**!

Expect opportunities to **receive airdrops in SUZ and other tokens**, and play in DeFi with more integrations!
