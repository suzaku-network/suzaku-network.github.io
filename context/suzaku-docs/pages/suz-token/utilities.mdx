# SUZ Token Utilities

## 1. Exposure to the Avalanche L1s Ecosystem

Contribute to the security of L1 networks and earn along the way.

Staking SUZ exposes holders to **incentives and potential airdrops** from Avalanche L1s and DeFi partners. By participating in the Suzaku ecosystem, SUZ holders gain exposure to the broader Avalanche L1 landscape and can benefit from the growth of multiple networks simultaneously.

## 2. sSUZ & Rewards

Stake SUZ to receive **sSUZ**, our Liquid Staking Token. Learn more about [SUZ staking and rewards](/suz-token/suz-staking).

It's your gateway to the full Suzaku ecosystem, providing liquid access to staking rewards while maintaining the flexibility to use your position in DeFi protocols.

## 3. Governance

Vote on **key decisions** shaping the future of the protocol.

SUZ = power. As the protocol matures, important decisions will be made through an open governance process where voter rights are determined by the amount of SUZ tokens staked and overall community engagement.

## 4. Native Token of Suzaku L1s

We are evaluating **launching infrastructure L1s** to further decentralize the ecosystem.

L1s developed or funded by the Suzaku Foundation will use **SUZ for staking and gas**. This creates real on-chain utility for the token beyond just governance and protocol participation.

A token designed for real on-chain use, SUZ will serve as the native token for infrastructure L1s that secure interoperability between Avalanche L1s.

## 5. And more...

Expect more utilities, including **protocol revenue sharing**, as Suzaku evolves.
