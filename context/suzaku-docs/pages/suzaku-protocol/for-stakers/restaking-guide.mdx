# Restaking Guide

## Deposit assets into Suzaku Collaterals

The first step to participate in Suzaku Restaking is to **deposit a supported asset into its Collateral contract**. This will give extra properties to the assets, allowing them to be used in the protocol.

Depositing assets into Collaterals will accrue [Suzaku Points](/suzaku-protocol/for-stakers/suzaku-points).

<Callout type="info">
  Learn more about Collaterals in the [Protocol
  Specifications](/suzaku-protocol/protocol-specs) section.
</Callout>

<Steps>

### Open the Suzaku dApp

The Suzaku dApp is available at [https://app.suzaku.network](https://app.suzaku.network).

### Connect your wallet

Click on the `Connect Wallet` button and select your preferred wallet. You will be asked to sign a message to accept Suzaku's Terms and Conditions.

![Connect Wallet](/img/suzaku-protocol/guide/suzaku-dapp-connect.png)

### Navigate to the Zap page

Use the navigation bar to go to the `Zap` page.

![Navigate to Zap](/img/suzaku-protocol/guide/suzaku-dapp-zap.png)

### Select the asset to deposit and the collateral to deposit into

In the `From` dropdown, select an token that you want to deposit. In the `To` dropdown, choose a collateralized token (e.g., `DC_sAVAX`) to receive in exchange.

<Callout type="info">
  You can also select a `Suzaku LRT` to gain `Suzaku Points` and more!
</Callout>

![Select Asset](/img/suzaku-protocol/guide/suzaku-dapp-zap-collateral.png)

### Enter the amount and Zap in

Enter the amount you want to zap and then click on the `Zap in` button.

### Navigate to the Dashboard page

Once the transactions are confirmed, you will see your Collateral position and your [Points](/suzaku-protocol/for-stakers/suzaku-points) on the Dashboard page.

![Navigate to Dashboard](/img/suzaku-protocol/guide/suzaku-dapp-dashboard.png)

</Steps>
