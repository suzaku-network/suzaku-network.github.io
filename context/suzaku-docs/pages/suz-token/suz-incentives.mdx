# SUZ Incentives

Out of transparency, this page details SUZ incentives that have been or will be distributed, and from what allocation the tokens come from.

## Staking Incentives

SUZ staking rewards are determined **yearly** and are distributed from the **Ecosystem and Community** allocation.

| Year      | Start Date | End Date   | SUZ Staking Rewards |
| --------- | ---------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| 2025-2026 | 2025-08-13 | 2026-08-12 | 1,500,000 SUZ       |
