# SUZ Token

Suzaku is a **permissionless protocol** allowing:

- Anyone to **stake their assets** to provide cryptoeconomic security
- Anyone to **curate staking strategies** for their delegators
- Anyone to **operate infrastructure** to decentralize L1 blockchains
- Anyone to **create their L1 and secure** their decentralization journey

As a vital component of the Layer 1 blockchains it supports, Suzaku must have a **clear path toward decentralizing** its governance and revenue flows.

We introduce the **SUZ token** to reduce dependency on the founding organization and promote protocol maturity. This utility token will **power the Suzaku protocol and its ecosystem**.
