# SUZ Token Distribution

**SUZ** will have a maximum supply of **100,000,000 tokens** that will be distributed as follows at TGE (Token Generation Event):

![SUZ Token Distribution.png](/img/suz-token/suz-token-distribution.png)

## Ecosystem & Community (31.255%)

**10% unlocked, 12-month linear vesting**

### Ecosystem Development

Reserved for **grants** to Suzaku-secured L1s or DeFi/infrastructure projects that expand and enrich the Suzaku ecosystem, as well as for **hackathons**.

### Community Incentives

Distributed to **SUZ stakers**, **liquidity providers**, and users performing **virtuous activities** within the broader Suzaku ecosystem.

## Airdrops (7.5%)

Rewards for **early Suzaku supporters**. They will be spread among different seasons, which will be communicated via official channels.

## Liquidity (10%)

**100% unlocked**

Used for initial **liquidity bootstrapping** and future SUZ CEX/DEX listings.

## Private & Public Sales (10.745%)

**Private: 30% unlocked, 9-month linear vesting**  
**Public: 100% unlocked**

Sold to **private and public investors** to finance the initial development of the Suzaku protocol.

**Note:** Unsold tokens will be reallocated to the Foundation Treasury.

## Core Contributors & Advisors (22%)

**18 months linear vesting**

Distributed to **team members** who created and developed the protocol, as well as **key advisors** who helped shape the project.

## Foundation Treasury (18.5%)

**15% unlocked, 24-month linear vesting**

Dedicated to **sustaining ongoing operations and future developments** of the protocol.
